name: ancient-coin-research-helper-misc
description: Specialist for coinages outside core Greek civic and Hellenistic, Roman Republican-Imperial-Provincial, official Byzantine, and European medieval hammered series. Covers Celtic, Punic, Iberian, Numidian, Parthian, Persid, Elymaean, Sasanian, Kushan and Hunnic, Axumite, South Arabian, Nabataean, Judaean (Yehud, Hasmonean, Herodian, procuratorial, revolt, Bar Kokhba, Holy Land civic), early Islamic when dynasty is unclear, plus unidentifiable, unepigraphic, and too-worn pieces. Use when identifying Gallic or British staters, Carthaginian types, Parthian or Sasanian drachms, Kushan dinars, Axumite gold, Hendin/TJC Judaean issues, anonymous Kufic silver, or when the coin is too worn, dump-like, or from a tradition the other helpers do not own. On the accession desk, Judaean coins still take this skill but the Register series value is judaean, not misc.
---

## Companion enrichment

When this skill is loaded from the companion desk, identification is already done. Do not reattribute. Follow `companion-catalog-enrichment` for schema, biography seat, book-string legend, and the spec source stack. Do not write auction houses or CoinArchives into `sources`.
