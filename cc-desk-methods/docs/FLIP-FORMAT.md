# Flip Format (public face)

Extracted from the FlipFormat sheet of the Accession Register.

Printed flip — public face
Line order (always)
1  Name, rank (required). Rank = office of the person on the obverse. For anonymous folles, line 1 is the issuing authority.
2  Year or date range the coin was struck
3  Mint city and mint mark
4  OBV: legend (and type only if the legend is absent or secondary)
5  REV: type and/or legend
6  Diameter + weight + die axis   example: 20mm 2.5g 6H
7  Denomination (for anonymous folles: AE Follis, Anonymous Class X)
8  Primary catalog reference (swap the corpus; do not add a ninth line)
Missing data = ?. Exception: title/rank is never blank or ? when the obverse person is known.
No condition, price, source, or notes on the flip. Those stay on Register.
Die axis always conventional clock format: 6H, 12H, 1H. Unknown = ?
